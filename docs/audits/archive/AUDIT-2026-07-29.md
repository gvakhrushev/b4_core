## F1 — exit-weight scaling defeated by `x = WAD − 1` (pool-weight integrity)

**High.** `initiateExit(WAD − 1)` withdrew ~100 % of a vault's capital while keeping its full
reported pool weight — a departed vault diluting every stayer. The scan produced **no patch**: its
two attempts either repriced ordinary partial exits (reversing a deliberate economic decision) or
rested on a caller-supplied post-exit base the owner can re-inflate, and the verifier's exploit
still worked. Its conclusion — *"closing it needs a measure the pool can verify itself"* — is
exactly the shipped fix.

**Fix (commit `2514fd9`).** `B4VaultOps._finalizeExit` calls `B4Pool.scaleWeight(id, keep)` on
**every** exit that follows a settle, lowering the already-reported weight and `totalWeight` by the
same `keep` the ledger uses — a pool-side measure, not a caller-supplied one. Confined to
`≤ reportDeadline` (disjoint from the claim window, D2/D3); every non-applicable case is a silent
`return` (permissionless crank path, no admin, H3). Regression: exit-weight proportionality
(TEST_PLAN §3b) + `invariant_weight_ledger_is_closed`.

## F2 — peak-anchor "slot squat" (over-levered short)

**High (error direction raises leverage).** A permissionless daily-close slot could suppress the
served `peakC`, raising the structural short's leverage. The scan **declined** to patch inside
`sampleAnchor` — it showed F2 (suppression) and the M-3 wick (inflation) are the same daily race
from two sides, and three existing pinned assertions made any in-place rule contradictory; the
choices were owner policy calls.

**Fix (commit `2514fd9`).** The value binding was separated from the density counter (SPEC §7b):
peak = max over daily **closes**, bound only inside `ANCHOR_CLOSE_WINDOW` and corroborated at **two
distinct close-days** (served `peakC` = second-highest close); the low side keeps its fail-safe
every-observation ratchet. Unconfirmed anchors are withheld as 0 → engine degrades to flat-`φ`.
Regression: `AnchorRatchet.t.sol`, `V9AnchorDensity.t.sol`, `V8C_PeakWick.t.sol`.

## F3 — permissionless `claimDeferred` races an in-flight Core→EVM return → permanent wedge

**High. The scan wrote a verified patch (`F3.patch`) that was never applied; closed here on
2026-07-30.**

`_unaccountedEvm` — the receipt proof for every Core→EVM leg — excluded `deferredPayoutTotal` from
`booked`, on the stated grounds that it "changes only inside settle / exit-finalize, both of which
require an idle engine." **That premise was false:** `B4VaultRecovery.opsClaimDeferred` (reached via
the permissionless, non-idle `B4Vault.claimDeferred`) also decrements it. A claim of `A` landing
during a live `ReturnDir`/`ReturnUsdc` lowered the EVM balance without lowering the measure, so
`received` fell permanently short of `evmNeeded`; with the Core source already debited, A7 keeps the
resend branch shut, so the intent could **neither complete nor resend**, `emergencyClearRecovery`
refuses it (`Recover*` kinds only), and every idle-gated entrypoint (settle, exit-finalize, all
recovery) dies on `_requireIdle()` — no admin to unstick it. **Not attacker-only:** `Keeper.crank`
reaches `crankVault` then `retryDeferred` on the same vault in one transaction, so an honest keeper
triggers it.

**Fix.** `_unaccountedEvm` now adds `deferredPayoutTotal[token]` to `booked` — a deferred payout is
value the vault holds but *owes*, so it is accounted, not "unaccounted". A claim then lowers `bal`
and `deferredPayoutTotal` by the same `A` and cancels exactly; payouts are *deferred* only inside
the idle-gated settle/exit, so the subtrahend cannot rise under a live leg either. `opsRecoverEvm`
already subtracted `deferredPayoutTotal` on its own path, so there is no double-count. Regression:
`test/unit/DeferredClaimReturnRace.t.sol` (fail-before confirmed: both the permissionless-claim and
honest-`Keeper` cases left the leg wedged on the pre-fix tree). Verified 2026-07-30: 462 tests pass,
fmt clean, storage-layout guard passes, all contracts within EIP-170 (`B4Vault` 141 B headroom).

## F4 — one-shot `settle` lets a third party pin another vault's weight at a trough

**Low; declined as an accepted residual.** The scan showed every code fix creates a worse residual:
uniform per-interval valuation reopens the C-1 Critical *by measurement* (patching `opsSettle` to
the locked price reproduced the exploit — 83.3 % of the basket), and every caller-restriction
variant either bricks `Keeper.settleVault`, hands a repeatable mint, or enables critical-grade
grief; no TWAP source exists (precompile reads are instantaneous).

**Mitigation shipped.** `B4Vault.snapshotNav` captures NAV and its price together, one-shot, confined
to the settlement day, narrowing the choosable instant from three days to one; the owner pre-empts
at `pointTime`. The remaining differential-timing residual is documented (SECURITY_MODEL §3);
per the scan, the accepted-residual clause should state that single-pass uniform pricing is an
operational expectation, not a code guarantee.
